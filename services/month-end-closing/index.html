A SMOOTH, ACCURATE CLOSE

Support shaped around your business

Month-end closing brings a month’s activity to a consistent, reviewed state. I review and reconcile the records you provide, follow up on missing or unusual items, and organize anything that needs your approval, so the month is ready for reporting or for your accountant’s review.

WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

What’s Included

10 Essential Tasks

  1. Review monthly transactions

    Confirm the month’s income, expenses, and transfers are recorded and categorized.

  2. Check categorization

    Review transaction categories for consistency and correct agreed misclassifications.

  3. Reconcile bank accounts

    Match recorded activity to bank statements and note any open items.

  4. Reconcile credit card accounts

    Verify card transactions and balances against the monthly statements.

  5. Review accounts payable

    Check unpaid bills and vendor balances for accuracy and timing.

  6. Review accounts receivable

    Review open invoices and customer payments against your records.

  7. Identify missing items

    Find missing transactions and documents and request what is needed.

  8. Investigate unusual balances

    Look into balances or activity that seem out of line and flag questions.

  9. Organize month-end adjustments

    Prepare agreed adjustments and supporting notes for your review and approval.

  10. Prepare reports or a summary

    Prepare financial reports or a month-end summary for your review.

Accurate records. Clearer decisions.

A consistent month-end close gives you a clearer picture of business performance and keeps every month’s records ready for review and planning.
