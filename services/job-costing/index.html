COSTS ORGANIZED BY PROJECT

Support shaped around your business

Job costing assigns expenses to the jobs or projects they belong to. Using the records, receipts, and timesheets you provide, I organize project costs so you can see what each job is costing, compare projects, and review the results with your team or accountant.

WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

What’s Included

10 Essential Tasks

  1. Set up job tracking categories

    Organize jobs, projects, or classes so costs can be assigned consistently.

  2. Record project expenses

    Record project-related expenses and assign each one to the correct job.

  3. Categorize material costs

    Separate material purchases by job and by type of cost.

  4. Track labor costs

    Allocate labor costs by job from the timesheets and payroll records you provide.

  5. Organize subcontractor costs

    Record subcontractor bills and payments against the right project.

  6. Monitor project purchases

    Track tools, supplies, and other purchases tied to each project.

  7. Review project transactions

    Check project-coded transactions for accuracy and flag questions.

  8. Compare costs by project

    Compare recorded costs across projects or against the budgets you provide.

  9. Prepare job cost reports

    Prepare reports showing recorded costs by job and by category.

  10. Organize project summaries

    Keep project financial summaries organized for review and planning.

Know your project costs. Plan with confidence.

Organized job costing shows where project money goes, makes cost overruns easier to spot, and gives you clearer numbers when reviewing completed jobs or planning new ones.
