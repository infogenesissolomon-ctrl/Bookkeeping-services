A CLEAR, ORGANIZED APPROACH

Support shaped around your business

Cleanup work is for businesses whose books have fallen behind, were set up inconsistently, or no longer match their bank and card statements. I review the records and access you provide, organize and correct what the documentation supports, and bring unclear items to you instead of guessing.

WHAT I CAN HELP WITH

What’s Included

10 Essential Tasks

  1. Review existing records

    Assess your current books, accounts, and the periods that are behind or incomplete.

  2. Organize transactions

    Sort and categorize uncategorized or misfiled transactions, transfers, and other activity.

  3. Identify missing information

    Find gaps such as missing transactions, statements, or receipts, and list what is needed.

  4. Fix incorrect or duplicate entries

    Flag duplicate or miscategorized entries and correct them where the records support it.

  5. Reconcile eligible accounts

    Reconcile bank and credit card accounts for the periods where statements are available.

  6. Categorize receipts and documents

    Match receipts and supporting documents to transactions and file them consistently.

  7. Review the chart of accounts

    Review your account structure and suggest cleanup so categories stay clear and consistent.

  8. Investigate discrepancies

    Trace unexplained differences and document the questions that need your input.

  9. Prepare updated records

    Bring the books up to date for the agreed periods, ready for your review.

  10. Summary and next steps

    Summarize the work completed, any open items, and recommended steps for ongoing upkeep.

Clear records. Easier review.

Books that are up to date and organized make it easier to track business activity, find supporting documents, and prepare reports when you need them.
